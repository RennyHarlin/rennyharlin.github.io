A life well-lived is a life well-planned. But, how do we plan for something that is uncertain ?

Everything about us is uncertain. This is a question, that has haunting me lately.

Over the years, my plans, todo lists, calendars, etc are upto no good because of sudden responsibilities that show up to disrupt my schedule. 

I can always stick to my plan, I can prioritize my plan and just let the other things that show up loose. 

Some of my colleagues do that. Overtime, they have built discipline and were able to progressively achieve their short term and long term goals.

But, I am in the other end of the spectrum, I just can't let things loose. Although, I perform well in the sudden responsibilities, but I lag behind in my plan. My plan is the one that is reponsible for career development, and the quality of life I am going to live after my undergraduate. 

The responsibilites that show up are related to my undergraduate course tests, assignments etc. I'm held accountable for these by my professors, so I just don't have the courage to let things loose.

But, my placement and higher studies plan. No one, holds me accountable :( 
    
That's where I think everyone should hold me accountable, not for mere test scores, cgpa, etc. At the end of the day, what's the point of a good cgpa with no real skills ?

I still haven't found the solution to this. Books tell me *self-discipline*, My heart tells me *study-partner*, My mind tells me *drop everything and focus on your career*, My melancholy tells me *focus on both*.

I have to find a solution to this soon. Time is running by, Opportunities are slipping by, Failure is heading by. Let's see if my end is happy story or a sad story.
