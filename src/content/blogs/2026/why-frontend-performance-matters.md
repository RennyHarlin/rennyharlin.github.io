# Why Frontend Performance Matters

Performance is not just a technical detail. It shapes how people perceive the product and whether they continue using it.

## Common benefits

- faster initial load times
- less friction on mobile devices
- cleaner user experience
- better SEO outcomes

## A simple rule

If your pages feel fast, users are more willing to explore. If they feel slow, even excellent content can get ignored.

This post can act as a practical example when testing blog cards, summaries, and sorting on the front-end.
