# Notes on Shipping Fast

Shipping is often more valuable than polishing endlessly. Teams move faster when they choose a clear goal and simplify the work required to reach it.

## A practical mindset

- start with the core problem
- put a simple version live early
- gather feedback quickly
- improve the details later

## Why it works

The fastest path to learning is usually to publish a version that works, collect reactions, and iterate. This keeps momentum high and avoids analysis paralysis.
