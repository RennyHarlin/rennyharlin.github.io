# Building a Portfolio Site

A portfolio site should make your work easy to understand at a glance. The best ones usually focus on clarity, visual rhythm, and a strong call to action.

## Structure matters

People usually scan before they read. That means your landing page should highlight:

- your best projects
- your expertise
- your contact information
- a link to your writing

## Keep it lightweight

Even a simple static site can feel premium when the content is well arranged and the loading experience is fast. Astro is a strong fit for this because it encourages clean structure and performance.

This sample is intentionally short so it can be used for testing layout and content rendering.
